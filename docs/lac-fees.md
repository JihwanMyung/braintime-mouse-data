# LAC monthly fees

LAC fees estimates a calendar month's usage in TWD. Billing options map exact room and optional rack IDs: Mouse clean room and Rat room locobox use cages, SPF uses mice, and Ani Beh is excluded. Daily rates multiply cage-days or mouse-days; monthly rates use the corresponding monthly quantities.

Rates are dated. An effective-from policy remains available prospectively until superseded. A snapshot-only rate observation applies only on its documented date. An observed rate does not prove its earlier effective date. Each options change and monthly revision appends a new husbandry event with provenance and a predecessor reference.

Current forecasts project the currently assigned Stock animals and occupied cages across the selected current month. Missing or ambiguous housing prevents a complete total. Previous-month estimates require dated billed quantities or occupancy totals covering the full billing month, with applicable rates. Missing coverage, isolated snapshots, unreviewed source-formula or ambiguous quantities, conflicting records, and snapshot-only rates show Unavailable. A dated snapshot may support a labeled forecast for the current month only. Source-formula quantities retain their provisional label. Recorded actual charges remain available independently of the estimate.

Monthly records hold invoice totals, source references, notes and quantity evidence. Compare the estimate with an invoice for the same explicitly identified billing month. Payment date alone does not establish a billing month. Error is abs(estimate - actual) / actual × 100. The default target is 5%, with a configurable maximum no greater than 10%. A zero invoice and nonzero estimate has undefined percentage error. Missing evidence is never counted as a successful comparison.

Options, actual charges and source caveats remain in the private colony database. No Grant workbook, invoice or lab contact data belongs in public website assets. Record editing follows the Fees capability for managers; the owner alone manages capabilities. History exports apply the existing Low (3 years), High (6 years) and Max limits to the billing month, with reviewed fields only in bounded reports.

Historical source snapshots do not establish full-month usage, so their estimates and error percentages are unavailable. Original observations and actual charges remain unchanged.

Estimated charges, recorded charges and confirmed payments are distinct. Existing imported Grant Sheet amounts remain recorded charges. Mark a payment as confirmed only when its amount and payment date are known; saving that distinction creates a new revision and never changes a historical source amount.

The owner can independently reveal estimates and actual amounts to Low, High and Max users. All six switches default to visible, preserving existing access; the owner always retains full access. Differences require both amounts. PostgreSQL applies the same policy to direct record reads, reports and writes. When either amount is hidden, the app receives a reviewed fee projection with neutral source-reference placeholders. It cannot edit fee records or source archives, and it cannot read raw audit snapshots, raw source archives, raw Animal Sheet cage snapshots or full backups, because those can contain financial cells. Sanitized cage-snapshot records retain only event metadata when either financial side is hidden. Ordinary animal management remains available under the user's existing role. Previously downloaded external files cannot be recalled.

Current Animal Sheet positions use the same protection. Restricted users receive room, rack, position, manager, identifiers and managed notes through a reviewed location projection. Raw source blocks and nested source evidence stay hidden. Editing a projected position preserves those hidden fields atomically and retains the ordinary version and permission checks. Daily source-change history and import-conflict details are also withheld; conflict identifiers and version metadata remain available.

Deploy `supabase/lac-fee-visibility.sql` **after** all domain, manager and export migrations. It wraps the final export and write-permission functions without changing their date or domain rules. Reapply it last after any future migration that replaces those functions. It can be rerun safely and preserves the owner's settings. The app verifies visibility before loading cached shared data, clears old raw caches when access is reduced, and clears shared caches on sign-out. Local-only personal workspaces remain separate.
