# Colony planning research

Reviewed 2026-10-14. This is a comparison of documented capabilities, not a product benchmark. The Braintime design recommendations informed this implementation; vendor claims do not validate our calculations or the colony's imported genotype labels.

## Documented tools and useful lessons

| Tool | What the official sources establish | Useful bounded lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SoftMouse | Custom gene/allele vocabularies, complex genotype records, pedigrees, mating/litter workflows, scheduling and cage cards. Genotype imports include a validation/preview step. | Keep source text and an explicit mapping preview. A custom label is not by itself a verified locus, insertion site or zygosity assay. The reviewed pages do not establish a linked-locus probability model. |
| Transnetyx Colony + AMI | Colony records and genotyping integration; AMI advertises project estimates, breeding-performance and pup-survival tracking, Mendelian/sex-ratio alerts, and recommendations users can review. | Separate observed yield from expected inheritance. Offer explainable assumptions and reviewable alerts before attempting automatic breeding optimization. Public marketing pages do not document enough mathematics to reproduce their predictions. |
| JAX Colony Management System | A historical relational colony/research-data system. JAX now explicitly marks JCMS unsupported and no longer developed. | Its separation of animals, pedigrees, experiments and controlled vocabularies is useful historical design context; it is not a supported replacement recommendation. |

Sources: [SoftMouse features](https://www.softmouse.net/colonyTempfeature.html), [SoftMouse genotype vocabulary](https://www.softmousefaq.com/Understanding%2BGenotypes%2Bin%2BSoftMouse), [genotype import preview](https://www.softmousefaq.com/Import%2BGenotype%2BResults), [Transnetyx Colony + AMI](https://www.transnetyx.com/services/colony), [current JCMS status](https://www.jax.org/research-and-faculty/resources/jax-colony-management-system), [original JCMS paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2844967/).

## Braintime design sequence

Items 1–3 are implemented in the first planner release. Item 4 records future validation work.

1. **A source-backed locus catalog and editable genotype worksheet.** Discover names in the user's loaded records and source archive; retain provenance. Curate exact allele identity separately from aliases. Preserve unknown states, assay limitations, and contradictory records. Catalog a reporter, a Cre driver and its insertion locus separately when evidence warrants that distinction; do not equate a promoter name with an insertion chromosome.
2. **A transparent single-cross calculator.** Start with diploid autosomal loci, explicit parental allele pairs, and declared independent assortment. Show every offspring genotype and its probability, target/control definitions, and assumptions. Keep inferred source parsing visibly unconfirmed. Block unsupported sex linkage and known linked loci rather than multiplying their probabilities. Hypothetical calls and plans must not rewrite animal records or create actual matings.
3. **A small planning notebook.** Save parent-call snapshots and the intended experimental/control genotypes; allow reviewed multi-generation steps. Distinguish conditional genotype frequency, expected number, a binomial attainment probability, and an operational forecast. A nominal litter size is an assumption, not a calendar prediction. Local breeding performance should inform later forecasts, with small samples and incomplete observations visible.
4. **Reviewable quality checks.** Flag incompatible parent/offspring calls for investigation without choosing among ambiguous parents or correcting evidence automatically. Compare birth, weaning and genotyping cohorts separately; selective sampling, survival and uncertain parentage can distort apparent Mendelian ratios. Rank eligible supplied pairs only, with reasons for exclusions, rather than claiming a global biological optimum.

JAX's complex-cross guidance recommends specifying exact experimental and control genotypes, considering chromosome location, background, viability and fertility, and working backwards from the required final cross. Its colony-planning guidance emphasizes facility-dependent breeding performance. Those support the sequence above, but not a guaranteed production date. [Complex breeding schemes](https://resources.jax.org/husbandry/strategies-for-creating-complex-breeding-schemes), [colony planning](https://www.jax.org/jax-mice-and-services/customer-support/technical-support/breeding-and-husbandry-support/colony-planning).

## Scientific limits the interface must retain

| Issue | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Alternative reporters at one locus | Ai32 and Ai96 are different `Gt(ROSA)26Sor` alleles, not independent loci. Represent `+/Ai32`, `+/Ai96` or `Ai32/Ai96` at one locus; reject a request for simultaneous Ai32/Ai32 and Ai96/Ai96 in an ordinary diploid animal. Imported separate positive/negative assay labels need reconciliation, not automatic conversion into a compound heterozygote. [JAX Ai32](https://www.jax.org/strain/012569), [JAX Ai96](https://www.jax.org/strain/024106). |
| Presence, zygosity and copy number | A transgene-positive endpoint assay may not distinguish hemizygotes from homozygotes. Hemizygous means one insertion-bearing homolog, not necessarily one molecular transgene copy. Do not infer 50% transmission from an unqualified positive label. [JAX qPCR explanation](https://www.jax.org/jax-mice-and-services/customer-support/technical-support/genotyping/jax-protocols-explained/qpcr-probe-and-endpoint-analysis), [example endpoint assay limitation](https://www.jax.org/Protocol?protocolID=40389&stockNumber=006054). |
| Linkage and phase | Separate loci on one chromosome require phase and recombination information. For a coupling-phase `AB/ab` parent, gametes AB and ab each have probability `(1-r)/2`, and Ab and aB each `r/2`. This is a Mendelian derivation, not an empirical estimate of `r`; physical distance alone is not a universal conversion. Unknown phase remains unresolved or becomes a labeled scenario. [MGI/JAX linkage account](https://informatics.jax.org/silverbook/chapters/7-2.shtml). |
| Inherited components versus functional deletion | A Cre/flox/reporter genotype does not establish target deletion. Reporter and target recombination can disagree. Keep floxed, recombined/deleted and wild-type alleles distinct; Cre-negative offspring can inherit a germline-recombined allele. [Liu et al., original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3696028/), [Luo et al., original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7377387/). |
| CreER and induction | Record induction exposure and timing separately from inherited genotype. Age, strain and treatment affect tamoxifen metabolism; some tested CreER/reporter combinations show recombination without induction. Do not supply a universal dose, washout or perfect on/off assumption. [Valný et al., original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5071318/), [Liu et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3696028/), [JAX induction guidance](https://www.jax.org/research-and-faculty/resources/cre-repository/tamoxifen). |
| Controls, background and driver effects | Offer explicit control definitions appropriate to the question, including Cre-negative floxed, driver-only and treatment controls where warranted. A generic WT label is insufficient. Parental driver sex can matter for germline recombination; paternal transmission is not universally protective. Confirm line-specific viability, fertility and target-tissue behavior. [JAX Cre-lox caveats](https://www.jax.org/news-and-insights/jax-blog/2016/may/are-your-cre-lox-mice-deleting-what-you-think-they-are). |

A useful test case is `Rosa26 Ai32/+ × Ai96/+`: ordinary single-locus inheritance gives one quarter each Ai32/Ai96, Ai32/+, Ai96/+ and +/+. An Ai32/Ai96 parent transmits one or the other allele, not both together. These are deductions under the stated model, not measured colony outcomes.

Recommended prediction label: **Expected inherited genotype frequency, conditional on the stated parental genotypes and inheritance model.** Keep functional recombination, live-born yield and experimental suitability as separately evidenced properties.
